Your information at Kenji’s

Privacy Policy

Updated November 3, 2026

This notice explains how Kenji’s Kitchen in Greater Lagro handles information when you create an account, place an order, or contact the eatery.

Account and verification

We use your name, username, email address, a protected password record, and email verification information to create and secure your account. We send registration and password reset codes to the email you provide. If you save favorites, we associate those choices with your account.

Orders and payments

To prepare your meal, arrange pickup or delivery, and respond to questions, we use your order number, items, quantities, instructions, contact number, and delivery address when needed. We keep transaction details such as the total, payment method, GCash reference when supplied, status, and receipt information.

Who uses this information

Staff use the details needed to prepare, hand over, and track your order. Delivery personnel need the contact and address details needed to reach you. Email services handle verification and receipt messages. If you pay through GCash, that provider handles the payment under its own privacy terms.

Information on your device

The storefront uses a session cookie for sign-in and browser storage for your basket and account display information. If you select Remember me, a separate cookie can keep you signed in on that device for up to 30 days. Signing out removes that remembered sign-in; changing your password invalidates remembered sign-ins. On a shared device, leave Remember me unchecked and sign out after ordering. You can clear this site’s data in your browser to remove locally saved information, including the basket. The neighborhood map in About us and external Facebook or Messenger links use their providers’ services.

How long records are needed

Account, order, and payment records serve different purposes. We retain records as needed to operate your account, fulfill orders, resolve payment questions, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact us about a specific record or a deletion request.

Your privacy choices

You can ask to access or correct your information, object to processing, or request deletion or blocking where applicable. Contact us using the details below. We may ask for enough information to confirm your identity before releasing or changing a record. You can also contact the Philippine National Privacy Commission about your rights or a complaint.
