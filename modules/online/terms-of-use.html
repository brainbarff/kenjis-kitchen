These terms apply when you use our online menu, customer account, and pickup or delivery service.

Your customer account

Choose a username and use an email address you can access. Registration requires a verification code sent to that address. Keep your password and verification codes private. If someone else uses your account, contact the eatery so we can look into it.

Placing an order

Check your items, quantities, special instructions, fulfillment method, and total before placing your order. The kitchen checks available stock at checkout. If an item runs out while you are browsing, you may need to adjust your basket. Use My Orders to follow the status of a submitted order.

Pickup and delivery

For pickup, collect your order at our Greater Lagro eatery. For delivery, provide an accurate address and a mobile number where we can reach you. If your address, contact details, or order needs to change after checkout, call us with your order number as soon as possible.

Payments and order concerns

Use one of the payment methods offered at checkout. For GCash, follow the payment instructions shown in your order and enter the reference from your receipt. If a payment appears twice, an item is missing, or you need to request a cancellation or refund, contact us with your order number so we can check its status and help.
